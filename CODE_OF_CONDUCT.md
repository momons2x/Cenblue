# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Cenblue community harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and focused on the project. Accept feedback gracefully, respect privacy, and avoid publishing another person's private information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, trolling, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
